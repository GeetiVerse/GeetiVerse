Explore ideas. Solve problems. Learn by doing.

GeetiVerse brings together consulting interests, interdisciplinary problem-solving, innovation discovery, and practical learning. It is a developing professional and innovation initiative, still taking shape.

Grounded in interdisciplinary systems thinking, data, and human-centered problem-solving, GeetiVerse aims to explore solutions that address meaningful societal challenges.

Through consulting, startup discovery, interactive learning, research, games, and shared resources, GeetiVerse provides a space to share and develop my ideas, involve people in exploring and testing solutions, exchange knowledge, and offer consulting support to those seeking it, all with the aim of turning ideas into practical solutions that empower people and create meaningful, sustainable, and resilient impact on communities and society.

About Ela

Initiator of GeetiVerse
Connecting Ideas, Systems, Data & Human Needs

My interdisciplinary background spans architectural engineering, design research, data science, and health informatics. I am interested in understanding complex systems and connecting ideas across disciplines to explore meaningful, human-centered solutions.

My doctoral research explored how design experts, including engineers and architects, think, communicate, and generate ideas during complex design processes, drawing on biological principles to inspire innovative solutions. This foundation continues to shape my approach to design thinking, interdisciplinary research, and systems-level problem-solving.

My work and research bring together systems thinking, human-centered design, analytics, and technology innovation. I view technology as part of a broader socio-technical system shaped by people, organizations, environments, and values.

Guided by ethics, equity, and empathy, and driven by enthusiasm for improving people’s quality of life, I am particularly interested in responsible AI that goes beyond technical performance to consider human needs, context, and meaningful human oversight. I explore how data, AI, and systems thinking can support better decision-making and address complex challenges in healthcare and society.

Beyond healthcare, I am interested in applying systems thinking and responsible innovation to sustainability, the circular economy, and resilient systems. Driven by curiosity about the deeper principles shaping human and technological systems, I explore how ideas from different disciplines can inform more thoughtful, responsible, and human-centered solutions.
